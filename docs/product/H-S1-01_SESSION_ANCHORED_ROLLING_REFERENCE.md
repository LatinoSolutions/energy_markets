H-S1-01 is a falsifiable **hypothesis derived from S1**, not S1 itself or a result. It asks whether causal relative price location against a session-anchored rolling reference improves procurement timing and economic outcome against CONTROL with identical sizing and execution. Later OOS evidence refutes or simplifies it if a frozen, valid comparison fails to show stable improvement, or improvement depends on an extremely narrow or unstable region of anchor `tau` or lookback `N`. Development calibration alone cannot establish a result.

The same scientific rule is available separately to Gas Monthly, Gas Quarterly, Power Monthly and Power Quarterly. Each has its own search-space hash, calibration, backtest, evidence, result and gates. There is no pooled score or shared economic verdict. The older SPEC §13.3 Gas Quarterly population describes the first P5 replay; Bru's later HYP-1 clarification expands this hypothesis's definition, without rewriting that replay or its receipts.

## Phase A rule and predeclaration

The mission-bound market session schedule supplies at most eight named local-time anchors and its source hash. Development availability rows must state the same mission, exact UTC timestamp, matching local session date/anchor, PIT availability and source hash; price, cost and outcome fields are rejected. `predeclareHS1SearchSpace` intersects that schedule with PIT-valid Development slots. A bounded predeclared lookback grid is `N ∈ {3,5,10,20}`; a point is eligible only when its anchor has at least `N+1` distinct available days before the Development boundary, supporting N previous observations and one decision day. This is a support rule, not economic selection. The space and every candidate carry content hashes. No anchor or N is declared a winner. Where metadata is absent or support is insufficient, the mission is `DATA_BLOCKED`; no candidate or AVAILABLE signal is fabricated.

For one candidate `(tau,N)`, at a decision timestamp matching `tau`, the arithmetic rolling mean uses exactly the **N prior** distinct session days at that same anchor. The current price is compared with that mean and is never included in it. Each historical observation must have been consumable at its own timestamp. `decisionPrice < reference` gives BUY eligibility; equality or a higher price gives WAIT. Continuous signed distance, normalized distance (when variance exists) and empirical percentile remain available. Warm-up below N gives `UNIDENTIFIABLE/ABSTAIN`, never an affirmative signal. Future PIT availability, a wrong anchor, duplicate days and malformed provenance fail closed. Additional thresholds and other reference families require a new predeclared version; they are not searched here.

CONTROL and H-S1-01 share the mission's obligation, opportunities, controller, execution contract and cost rules. On an eligible BUY, H-S1-01 returns CONTROL's requested quantity verbatim; on WAIT/ABSTAIN it requests zero and leaves Procurement State to the common controller. The historical 12 MW/day cap is a mission constraint where applicable, never signal sizing. Neither this contract nor the fixtures run calibration, backtests or OOS. Economic interpretation remains HOLD until mission-specific audit, reservation, frozen configuration, execution parity and comparable backend evidence pass their gates.
